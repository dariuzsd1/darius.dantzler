Before this, expense reimbursements and monthly stipends for interns at our research organization ran on email threads, spreadsheets, and manual bank transfers, with no consistent record of who approved what or when someone actually got paid. I built a structured web app to replace that: interns submit requests with receipts, I review and approve or reject them as treasurer, and our boss gets a simple pay list to mark items as paid once he's sent the money. The app never touches or moves money itself. It only tracks and presents what needs to be paid.

**Who uses it**

About 30 interns, plus two other roles: me as treasurer, and our boss, who handles the actual bank payments. Each role sees only what it needs. Interns manage their own requests and bank details, I get the full review dashboard and totals, and the boss gets a stripped-down pay list with just the amount and account details, since he needed something radically simple given how disorganized the old workflow was.

**How I built it**

I have no coding background. I acted as treasurer and product owner, defining the requirements from a treasury and HR controls perspective, and built the app by working directly with Claude Code. I wrote a formal working agreement (a CLAUDE.md file) that encoded the security rules, build order, and guardrails up front, then had it walk me through every setup step click by click. The stack is Supabase (Postgres with Row Level Security) for the backend, Google OAuth for login, and Vercel or Netlify for hosting, all on free tiers.

**Controls I'm proud of**

IBAN and account holder data are encrypted and visible only to the intern, me, and the boss at the payment step. Paid records are immutable rather than editable or deletable, so there's a clean audit trail. Monthly stipends run alongside reimbursements, with a treasurer-editable rate and a lightweight four-eyes check before payments generate. I also commissioned a visual redesign with a dark mode, Revolut-inspired interface, since the app gets used daily and needed to feel trustworthy, not like a spreadsheet with a login screen.
