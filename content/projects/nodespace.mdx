Every satellite operator runs into the same wall eventually: getting frequency spectrum filed and cleared through the ITU is slow, manual, and built for a world with far fewer operators competing for the same orbital slots and frequency bands. NodeSpace started from that observation: a SaaS concept built to handle ITU radiofrequency spectrum filing and spectrum-rights management for operators who'd rather build satellites than fight paperwork.

**What I did**

As venture lead, I ran the business side of the project from the ground up:

- **Value proposition**: defined what the product actually solves and why an operator would pay for it instead of handling filings the old way
- **Market case**: built the argument for why this problem is worth solving now, not shelved as a someday idea
- **Investor pitch**: presented the business case live to an audience of 300+ investors and space professionals

**Why it matters**

SpaceHub is research into how the New Space ecosystem works. NodeSpace is the applied version: taking a real regulatory bottleneck in the space industry and building an actual business case to fix it. That's the skill this project demonstrates, not just understanding how the space industry operates, but knowing how to build something that solves what's broken in it.
