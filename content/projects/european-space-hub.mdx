European Space Hub works in Europe's New Space sector, and I was recruited onto the team as an intern in June 2026. Fuller detail on the organization is being confirmed and will be filled in.

**My role**

I am not just another intern on the roster. I help coordinate the other interns and manage the finances that keep the operation running: tracking expenses, reimbursements, and stipends, and making sure the money side stays organized and accountable.

**The tool that came out of it**

That financial-management responsibility is exactly what led me to build the Intern Treasury Portal, the reimbursement and stipend app you'll find elsewhere in these projects. The messy process I was handling by hand became the problem the app was built to solve.

**Why it matters**

Space security and sustainability work doesn't happen without a healthy commercial ecosystem underneath it, and that ecosystem doesn't run without people willing to handle the unglamorous operational and financial work that keeps an organization functioning. That's the part I own here.
