[European Space Hub](https://www.eurospacehub.com/) is a digital platform and innovation ecosystem connecting Europe's space and aviation sector: universities, research centers, accelerators, and industry, working together on collaboration and entrepreneurship. It was founded by a group that includes VilniusTECH, the International Space University, Complutense University Madrid, the Lunex Foundation, and Collabwith Group, and is backed by the European Commission through the EIT Higher Education Initiative. I was recruited onto the team as an intern in June 2026.

**My role**

I'm not just another intern on the roster. Two things sit on my plate:

- **Operations and finance**: I help coordinate the other interns and manage the money that keeps the organization running, tracking expenses, reimbursements, and stipends, and making sure the financial side stays organized and accountable.
- **France branch development**: I've been tasked with standing up a branch in France, building the collaboration and innovation infrastructure that will connect France's space sector into the wider European Space Hub network.

**The tool that came out of it**

The financial-management side of the role is exactly what led me to build the Intern Treasury Portal, the reimbursement and stipend app you'll find elsewhere in these projects. The messy process I was handling by hand became the problem the app was built to solve.

**Why it matters**

Space security and sustainability work doesn't happen without a healthy commercial ecosystem underneath it, and that ecosystem doesn't run itself. It needs people handling the unglamorous operational and financial work, and people willing to go build new connections where none exist yet. Both of those are the parts I own here.
